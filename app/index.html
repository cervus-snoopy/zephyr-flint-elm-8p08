Save discipline

  • There is no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (10 saves) and rotate the other 5 slots for free-roam cleanup — PowerPyx.
  • Cheats disable trophies and saving, and cheats only unlock after the story — keep them off the platinum save. PowerPyx, GTA Spotter.
  • Optional missions with an hourglass icon expire, but none of them is needed for a trophy — PowerPyx.
  • Some quest-givers go quiet for a while mid-story (Kenton after Chapter 1, for example). All trophy-related characters come back — PowerPyx.

How this guide was built

  • Official names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them. Five trophies have no confirmed method in any published guide yet — those say so plainly instead of guessing.
  • Earn rates beside each trophy come from Exophase, as of 20 Nov 2026, and cover its tracked players only.
  • Counts in the banner are computed live from the guide data: one cited claim per guide step, and a claim counts as backed by 2+ sources when its step names two or more guides.
  • Guide text is written in our own words for this guide. No Rockstar logo, artwork or game screenshot is used anywhere.
